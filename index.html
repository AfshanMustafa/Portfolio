About

Research-led design for people who run on decisions.

I'm a Lead Product Designer focused on enterprise applications with AI-powered workflows. I research and design for many personas in one system, from corporate executives to frontline workers, and I build AI-driven experiences that feel like part of the tool, including conversational chat embedded in the workflow.

My path runs through IBM's CIO design team, 7-Eleven's mobile apps and enterprise operations platforms. Across all of it, I turn complicated, fragmented information into interfaces that are calm, clear and fast to act on.

How I work

Start with the decision

Fragmented signals only matter once they become a clear, prioritized next step. I design the path from data to action.

Design for every persona

From corporate executives to frontline workers, each role gets its own journey, language and level of detail.

AI that fits the workflow

Recommendations, scanning and conversational help sit inside the tools people already use, not in a separate place.

Prototype to prove it

Clickable prototypes and design-system-ready specs let teams test ideas early and build with confidence.
