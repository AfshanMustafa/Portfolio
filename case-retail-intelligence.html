Case study · Retail · Analytics

Retail Intelligence

Decision-ready analytics for retail partners, giving everyone from corporate leaders to store teams one clear view.

View Figma file →

Role

Lead Product Designer

Platforms

Desktop, tablet and mobile

Scope

Research and experience design

The challenge

Retail insight is only useful when it reaches the right person in the right form. A corporate executive and a frontline associate need very different views of the same business.

My role

I led research and design for the experience across personas, from corporate roles to frontline workers.

Process

01

Understand

Mapped the personas and the decisions each one makes.

02

Frame

Defined what each persona needs to see, and what can wait.

03

Design

Designed role-specific views across desktop, tablet and mobile.

04

Validate

Reviewed prototypes with stakeholders and refined the experience.

Key design decisions

One system, many views

Each persona gets a view tuned to their decisions while sharing one consistent language.

Insight first, detail on demand

Headlines are always visible, with deeper analysis one step away.

Responsive by role

Frontline workers get fast mobile views and executives get dense desktop views.

Deliverables

  • Persona-based experience design across devices
  • Clickable prototypes for stakeholder review
  • A consistent component language across roles

What this taught me

Clear enterprise design comes from deciding what matters most for each person, and then making everything else easy to reach.
