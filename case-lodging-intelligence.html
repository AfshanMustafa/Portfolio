Case study · Dashboard · AI

Lodging Intelligence

An AI-driven dashboard that turns fragmented operational signals into prioritized, GM-level decisions for hotels.

View Figma file →

Role

Lead Product Designer

Platforms

Desktop dashboard

Scope

Vision, prototype, design system

The challenge

Hotel general managers receive operational signals from many places at once. The challenge was to convert that noise into a short list of decisions a GM can trust and act on.

My role

I defined the product direction and designed the experience, from the first problem-space work through a navigable prototype.

Process

01

Vision

Wrote a 14-point product vision and defined the problem space before any screens.

02

Foundation

Designed the homepage and core layout on the existing Ecolab design system.

03

Prototype

Built a fully clickable seven-screen prototype for testing and stakeholder demos.

04

Refine

Iterated on priorities, insights and site views through repeated review.

Key design decisions

Priorities over metrics

The dashboard leads with what needs attention now, ranked for a GM, instead of a wall of numbers.

Four clear destinations

Overview, Priorities, Insights and My Site give each question its own place.

Built on the design system

Colors, type and component rules come from the shared system, so the product feels native and scales.

Deliverables

  • A 14-point product vision and problem-space definition
  • A seven-screen clickable prototype covering Overview, Priorities, Insights and My Site
  • Desktop designs at 1440px built on the design system

What this taught me

Clear enterprise design comes from deciding what matters most for each person, and then making everything else easy to reach.
