Case study · Mobile · Web · MVP

Laundry IQ

A mobile-first product that helps laundry teams scan stains, get AI product recommendations and see the savings behind every decision.

View Figma file →

Role

Lead Product Designer

Platforms

Mobile app and manager web view

Scope

Research, journeys, V1 design

The challenge

Laundry operations produce a constant stream of small decisions: which product to use, whether a result is on track, and where savings are being lost. Managers and workers needed the same system but very different views of it.

My role

I led UX for the MVP, from the research foundation through the V1 mobile experience, working closely with product and engineering.

Process

01

Research

Set up a shared research board as a living document for the whole design team.

02

Journeys

Built journey maps for two personas, the Laundry Manager and the Laundry Worker, and refined them iteratively.

03

Scope

Defined a focused V1 around the stain scanner and AI product recommendations.

04

Design

Designed the worker mobile app flows and the manager experience around savings and drill-down.

Key design decisions

Two personas, two journeys

The manager journey runs six phases: Login & Overview, Review Savings, Scan Dashboard, Drill Down, Make Decisions and End of Day. The worker journey stays short and task-first.

Scanner as the core moment

The stain scanner anchors V1, with AI recommendations arriving right where the worker needs them.

A living research board

One shared board keeps findings, journeys and decisions visible to the whole team as the MVP evolves.

Deliverables

  • Journey maps for the Laundry Manager and Laundry Worker personas
  • A V1 laundry worker mobile app covering the stain scanner and AI product recommendations
  • A shared research and UX board for the design team

What this taught me

Clear enterprise design comes from deciding what matters most for each person, and then making everything else easy to reach.
